## The Backstory

At Architrave, I led two multidisciplinary product teams totalling 10 people across backend, frontend, DevOps, and QA.

The technical work mattered, but the recurring leadership challenge was alignment: keeping ownership clear, connecting the roadmap to business outcomes, and helping different disciplines make decisions together.

## My Philosophy in Action

I worked to remove friction rather than centralise every decision. That meant clarifying responsibilities, coaching individuals, creating space for technical discussion, and making the reason behind priorities more visible.

**Engineering leadership is less about dictating technology and more about creating the conditions in which capable people can do their best work.**

## The Hard Truths

Organisational change takes longer than technical change. I initially underestimated how much repetition and informal communication a new way of working needs.

With hindsight, I would spend more time explaining the “why” before introducing the “how,” and create more frequent, low-stakes conversations before formal decisions.
