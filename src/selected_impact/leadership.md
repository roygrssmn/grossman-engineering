## The Backstory

As the organization scaled, silos formed between development, QA, and operations. Technical debt accumulated, and the alignment between technical roadmaps and stakeholder objectives began to deteriorate. Delivery dates were consistently missed, creating friction across the organization.

## My Philosophy in Action

I built and refined the cross-functional communication and established consensus to ensure production-grade reliability. By fostering a culture of ownership and providing direct mentorship to team leads, we broke down communication barriers. **I believe that engineering leadership is less about dictating technology and more about removing obstacles so talented people can do their best work.**

## The Hard Truths

Changing organizational culture takes much longer than changing technology. I initially underestimated the friction of transitioning from a legacy mindset to agile workflows. In hindsight, I would have over-communicated the "why" behind the strategic changes before introducing the "how," utilizing more watercooler talks to drive alignment.
