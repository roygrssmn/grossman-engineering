## The Backstory

As the organization scaled rapidly, silos formed between development, QA, and operations. Technical debt was accumulating, and alignment between engineering output and business objectives was deteriorating. Delivery dates were consistently missed.

## My Philosophy in Action

I implemented cross-functional squads and established clear quality gates. By fostering a culture of ownership and providing direct mentorship to team leads, we broke down communication barriers. I believe that engineering leadership is less about dictating technology and more about removing obstacles so talented people can do their best work.

## The Hard Truths

Changing organizational culture takes much longer than changing technology. I initially underestimated the friction that would come from transitioning legacy teams to new agile workflows. In hindsight, I would have over-communicated the "why" behind the changes before introducing the "how."
