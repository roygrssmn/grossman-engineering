## The context

At JFrog, I worked as a technical lead in a developer-platform environment serving roughly one billion downloads per month and holding about 800,000 packages across 20 formats.

At that scale, reliability is not an abstract quality target. Small gaps in test coverage, release confidence, or operational ownership can affect a very large downstream developer ecosystem.

## My contribution

My work centred on quality and test strategy for the platform:

- Set direction for technical quality and test architecture
- Helped teams reason about risk across package formats and platform changes
- Connected automated testing with release and operational feedback
- Participated in on-call responsibility and production learning

## What it reinforced

Developer infrastructure needs quality practices that account for scale, compatibility, and failure recovery—not only feature correctness. That experience shaped how I now approach platforms, delivery systems, and AI-enabled products: reliability has to be designed into the operating model.
